# Equilibrium Energy

> Equilibrium Energy is a venture-backed (DCVC) clean energy technology company building PowerOS, an agentic AI platform for the power and energy sector. PowerOS unifies fragmented enterprise data, models, and systems under a common ontology so power companies can deploy power-specific AI — enterprise agents, portfolio and trading application suites, and core services for optimization, forecasting, risk, and grid/market fundamentals — with guardrails they control. Its Mission Control product is in invite-only beta. There is no public developer API at this time.

## Product
- PowerOS — The Agentic AI Platform for Power: integrate existing data, models, and systems; leverage power-specific AI, models, and services.
- Enterprise Agents: Trader Copilots, Research Copilots, App Co-Developers, Data Ingestion, Data Transformations.
- Application Suites: Portfolio Management, Portfolio Construction, Development Workbenches, Middle & Back Office.
- Core Services: Optimization, Forecasting, Risk, Grid & Market Fundamentals, Trading.
- Connective Intelligence: Enterprise Data Ontology, Enterprise Code Frameworks.
